Export MetaForge progress to ARLO

Drag the button below onto your bookmarks bar (Chrome or Firefox). Then open your MetaForge Raider Profile (after Sync) and click the bookmark.

Export to ARLO

Drag this → bookmarks bar

  1. Start ARLO (import server on 127.0.0.1:17832).
  2. On MetaForge, Sync your Raider Profile.
  3. Click Export to ARLO while on metaforge.app.

ARLO must be running for one-click import. If it is not, the bookmark downloads account-sync.json instead.
